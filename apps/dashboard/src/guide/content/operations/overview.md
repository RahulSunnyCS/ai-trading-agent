## What it is for

The landing screen. It answers the questions worth asking every morning, one card each, and
every card links to the screen with the detail.

## The cards

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Market | Whether NSE is open, and the next session. |
| Feed | How fresh the live market feed is (used by the paused live engine). |
| Fyers token | Whether today's [Fyers login](glossary:fyers-token) is valid, and when it expires. Without it the evening run cannot collect data. |
| Paper trading | Status of the paused paper-trading engine. |
| Weekly momentum signal | Whether this week's signal has been produced and sent. |
| Options Lab evening job | How the last [evening run](glossary:evening-run) went. |
| Credits | Your [credits](glossary:credits), when billing is on. |

## Common questions

**A card is red. What now?** Click its link. For a failed job, the job's log in
[Jobs](app:/jobs) usually says why; for an expired token, see
[Broker logins](guide:operations/broker-logins).

**Can I land somewhere else?** Yes: Settings › Defaults › Landing tab.
