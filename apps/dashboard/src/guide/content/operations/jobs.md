## What it is for

Every recurring job the owner's laptop runs, in one table: when it runs, when it ran last and
how that went, and its log. You can also run a job now.

## Before you start

The jobs run on the owner's laptop through a scheduler service. If the screen says
**Scheduler not reachable**, the laptop (or its scheduler) is off; nothing is lost, jobs catch up
when it is back.

## The table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Job | Its id, such as `options-daily`, and the first sentence of what it does (hover for the rest). |
| Schedule | When it runs, in IST: "trading days 15:45", "Fri 14:40", "1st Sunday of the month 10:00". |
| Next run, Last run | When it will run next; when it last ran and whether it succeeded. |
| Group | Jobs in the same group never run at the same time (for example, two jobs that write the same data). |
| Needs | What it depends on, such as the laptop or a broker login. |
| Action | **Run now**. Some jobs ask you to confirm first. |

Click a job to read, under the table, what it does in full and the last 200 lines of its latest
log. The [Overview](guide:operations/overview)'s Jobs card lists the jobs that failed in the last
24 hours.

## The main jobs

| Job | What it does |
|---|---|
| `morning-open` (trading days 08:00) | The morning as one chain: each step starts when the one before it ends, and a step that fails **does not stop the steps that do not depend on it**. In order: (1) `angel-check`: one Angel One login, nothing fetched, to check the evening run's fallback source works; it never stops the rest, and a login Angel One refused is not sent again for 15 minutes (wrong attempts count toward a lockout). (2) `quantiply-login`: Quantiply through Angel One, and Kite when it is switched on, with the passwords in the repo `.env`; a part that fails for a reason another machine could fix is tried again on GitHub's "Daily broker login", that part only, and a second message links the run (a refused password or PIN is never sent again). (3) `quantiply-deploy` (**real money**): on each Quantiply account it keeps the clientwise MTM SL at the figure in [Settings](app:/settings) (set only when it differs, read back), then switches on today's algos the evening upload made, sets each to your broker account and reads the list again to check only those changed; it runs only if the Quantiply login passed, and the 09:19 `morning-topup` switches on what is left. Quantiply is switched on at about 08:06, **before** AlgoTest's window opens. (4) `algotest-login` waits for 08:16, AlgoTest's window, then logs in the AlgoTest brokers and Quantiply through Shoonya. (5) `algotest-catch-up`: a list you switched a row to after 16:40, which no row had then, is exported, uploaded and set up first. (6) `algotest-activate` (**real money**): activates last evening's list on AlgoTest's /live, one instance per AlgoTest block that uploads it; only if the AlgoTest login passed; a strategy already deployed is left alone and one whose entry minute has come is reported, never activated late. (7) `morning-message`: ONE Telegram message with the logins and each Quantiply account's MTM SL and what was switched on (and why, when nothing was). Logins and activations are never retried and a step that was cut off is never repeated unasked: **Resume** refuses and tells you to check by hand. A step past its last time (the Angel One check 09:10, Quantiply's steps 09:05, AlgoTest's 12:40) is not run, and the alert names it. A stop hold or a pause holds the two switch-on steps and the catch-up; the logins and the message still run. Once the Mac has run, GitHub's own 08:15 backup does nothing that day. The Jobs page's step panel shows each step. Needs `QUANTIPLY_DEPLOY=on` / `ALGOTEST_DEPLOY=on`, **Automated deployment** and a block uploading a list for the switch-ons. |
| `fyers-login-reminder` (trading days 08:05) | When Fyers does not accept today's token, a Telegram reminder with a link to [Broker logins](app:/brokerLogins) on the hosted dashboard, so you can log in from your phone. Silent otherwise. |
| `close-out` (trading days 15:30) | One chain of steps after the close. Each starts when the one before it ends (a clock time only where it matters), and a step that fails **does not stop the others**: the alert is one message naming each failed step with its own fix, and you can **Resume** it from the Jobs page (steps that passed are not run again). In order: (1) `fyers-login-recheck`: the Fyers reminder again, before the evening collection, if you still have not logged in (not after 15:45). (2) the 15:35 AlgoTest deploy check (not after 15:45). (3) `live-capture` (from 15:40): Reads, on AlgoTest and Quantiply, every strategy that ran today on the platforms and brokers [Settings](app:/settings) has on: the tool's own and the ones you added by hand ([discretionary](glossary:discretionary-trade)), with the live ₹, the multiplier and the fills where the platform shows them. It only reads, never changes anything. The 16:00 result message then shows each list's live result beside paper, with the [slippage](glossary:slippage) (your message only, never the friends' group). Both platforms keep a day's results after its strategies are deleted, so a day it missed is read the next evening (up to 5 trading days back). Platforms or brokers switched off in Settings are not read, so a trade you add there is not seen. (4) `quantiply-disable`: Switches off, on Quantiply, the algos the tool made for today that are still on; its message comes only when something went wrong (Telegram alerts: failures only). A Quantiply algo that stays on runs again on the same weekday next week. Only the tool's own algos, and only once they have finished. This is the safety net, so it always runs, even with `QUANTIPLY_DEPLOY` or **Automated deployment** off. (5) the Angel One capital reading (from 15:50). (6) `live-capture-retry` (from 15:55): Reads again only what the 15:40 read missed, until about 16:30. If the day is still not read, you get one alert, and the next evening reads it. |
| `morning-topup` (09:19) | The Quantiply top-up and its enable, in order, each with its own retries: first create any pick of today's entry that last night's upload does not have (not after 09:49; a failed top-up does not stop the enable), then `quantiply-deploy-topup`: After the 09:19 top-up: switches on any of today's strategies the 08:10 switch-on missed, and switches off one the day's entry does not have (if it has not started). Since the day's entry is last evening's list (9 Nov 2026), it is normally quiet. An algo that starts within 90 seconds is left off and reported as too late. It runs up to 3 hours late after a sleep, but only the enable step then. |
| `kite-login-reminder` (Fri 13:30) | When Kite is the Momentum holdings source and you have not logged in to Kite that day, a Telegram reminder to log in on [Broker logins](app:/brokerLogins) before the 14:15 orders, with the link for a phone login. Silent otherwise. |
| `kite-login-reminder-monday` (trading Mondays 08:45) | The same before the Monday open. |
| `morning-summary` (09:00) | One Telegram message, **only when something needs a look** (a failed check, or a pause in Settings): on a clean day the `morning-open` message (about 08:20) is your confirmation. It says: did the logins work, is the data current. Its Fyers line asks Fyers whether today's token works, and says to log in on [Broker logins](app:/brokerLogins) if not. It also lists the previous trading day's jobs that ran late because the laptop was asleep at their time ("Ran late after sleep"). |
| `trade-monitor` (09:04 to 15:30) | Watches the day's real trades and **only reads**: it never clicks, changes or squares off anything. Three parts, each off until its switch in the laptop's `.env` is `on`: from 09:05 to 09:10 the free margin of each broker account against what the day's picks need, roughly (`MARGIN_CHECK`); at 09:25 one line per platform, such as "AlgoTest T1 3/3 · Quantiply Core 3/3 ✅", saying whether each holds exactly the day's picks at 1 lot on the right broker, with what is missing or extra (`DEPLOY_CHECK`); and from 09:25 to 15:30 the combined profit or loss of the day on both platforms, read every minute, with a warning at -₹5,000 and an alert at -₹8,000, each sent once a day (`LOSS_ALERTS`). Between 09:10 and 09:25 it is logged out of both platforms, so it never gets in the way of the deploys. If it cannot read a platform it says so once ("cannot read"), and that platform is left out of the total. If the laptop or the scheduler restarts during the session, it starts again. |
| `evening` (15:45) | The [evening run](glossary:evening-run) as one chain: each step starts when the one before it ends, so nothing waits for a clock time and the uploads cannot start before the data is in. In order: (1) `collect` the day from Fyers when you are logged in (otherwise from Angel One, and says which in its Telegram message), (2) `rotation-update` stores the rotation results, (3) `derived-rebuild` builds any missing 5-minute snapshots and IV tables, (4) `t1-update` stores the T1 parallel track's results (a problem here sends its own alert and never stops the rest). Steps 1 to 3 retry through the evening (17:15, 18:45, 20:15) and stop the chain if they cannot finish. (5) `result`, never before 16:00: one Telegram message with the day's net paper gain for Adaptive and the T1 track (one lot per strategy, after costs), the month so far, how far T1 is below its high and what share of the last two years' days were as bad or worse, and tomorrow's strategies in full. If a result is not complete it tries again every 5 minutes and at 16:25 sends what exists. (6) `algotest-export`, `algotest-upload`, `algotest-setup`: tomorrow's picks into AlgoTest as saved strategies, replacing what the tool put there, with execution set up (never deployed). (7) `quantiply-export`, `quantiply-upload`: tomorrow's picks into Quantiply as algos, deleting the finished ones (never switched on). Each upload does nothing until its switch is on. (8) `t1-catch-up` and `t1-result`: stores any T1 day still missing and sends the T1 part of the result if it was not ready. A step that fails does not stop the steps that do not need it; the alert names each failed step with its own fix, and **Resume** on this page runs what did not pass. The **Apply now** buttons in [Settings](app:/settings) run only one platform's upload steps of this chain. |
| `options-rotation-pick` (08:45) | Records the day's rotation entry before the first entry minute, then the T1 track's in its own journal, from last evening's list: the strategies the 16:00 message sent for today, which the evening uploads put on AlgoTest and Quantiply. The evening list is what counts (since 9 Nov 2026): there is no 09:16 re-pick and no morning Telegram message. If the results changed overnight and a fresh ranking would differ, the evening's list is still the one recorded. You get an alert only if it fails, and the job tries again until 09:15. |
| `rotation-live-board` (09:18 to 15:31) | Runs the day's Adaptive picks, Classic and Tuned, through the backtest on Angel One's 1-minute prices so far, every 5 minutes and whenever you press Refresh on the [Live board](app:/optionslab/rotation). It logs in to Angel One once for the day and only reads: nothing is sent, traded or added to the database. When Angel One throttles a refresh, the board keeps the last figures and tries again 5 minutes later. |
| `momentum-preview` (Fri 14:40) | The momentum preview on live prices. |
| `momentum-final` (Fri 16:45) | The momentum final signal. |
| `momentum-stock-ingest` (Fri 19:30) | Refreshes NSE stock data, then the stock-based finals. |
| `momentum-journal-check` (Fri 21:00) | Checks the journal recorded every favourite. |
| `momentum-live-rules` (Fri 21:30) | Checks your live-money rules (drawdown, trailing, the money gate) and messages you. A rule that is hit always sends; it never trades. |
| `momentum-history` (Fri 22:00) | Checks that the stored market history up to a fixed Friday (the anchor: two to six weeks back; it moves forward after clean checks, and stays put while a change is unaccepted) has not changed since it was recorded: a restated close, replaced stock bars, a corporate action added with a past date. New weeks never trip it. It messages you only when something changed, naming each series and the earliest year that moved; backtest results that read those series may have moved too. The change keeps being reported each Friday until it is accepted (`mbt verify history --accept --reason "..."`). Silent when nothing changed. If a change could not be messaged (Telegram off or unreachable), the job shows as failed here. |

The monthly margin reminder and the yearly credential reminder stay their own jobs. |

## Missed vs failed

- **Catch-up:** if the laptop was asleep at a job's time, the job runs when it wakes.
- **Missed:** the job could not run in its window at all. Telegram says so.
- **Failed:** it ran and returned an error. The log says why. Failures and missed runs always
  alert, whatever your Telegram settings.

## What it does not tell you

Whether a successful job produced *sensible* data. That is what the quiet checks and the
morning summary are for.
