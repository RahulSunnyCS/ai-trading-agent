## What it is for

The rule ranks all 298 variants every morning and keeps three. Nothing in it says the three must be
spread across families, indexes or the day, so a list can slide toward, say, NIFTY Widesl at the
open for weeks without any single pick looking odd. This card counts what the focus list has been
holding and tells you when the last four weeks are unlike its own past.

It counts picks, not P&L. It never changes a list, a weight or the journal.

## The bars

For each of four ways of cutting the picks, three bars: the share of the list's core picks in each
category over **P1**, the **last 63** sessions and the **last 21** sessions.

| Cut | Categories |
|---|---|
| Family | Widesl, Dir |
| Index | NIFTY, SENSEX |
| Strike method | Widesl OTM1, Widesl closest premium, Dir ATM, Dir ITM1 |
| Start band | 09:17–10:02, 10:17–12:02, 12:17–14:02, 14:17–15:17 |

Only the three core picks a day are counted; the Buy add-on has its own row in the table. Point at
a segment for its pick count.

## The flag

A segment of the last-21 bar is outlined in amber, and the dimension names it, when its share is
outside the 10th to 90th percentile of that list's own rolling 21-session shares over P1. That is a
band taken from the list's own history, not a fixed number of points, so what the list has always
done is not flagged. Its "usual range" is printed beside the flag.

## The table

| Row | What it counts over the last 21 sessions |
|---|---|
| Distinct variants picked | How many different variants were held at all. |
| Most-picked variant | Times the single most-held variant was picked, and which one. |
| Days all three in one start band | Sessions whose core picks all start in one band. |
| Days the Widesl minimum applied | A Dir pick was replaced to keep two Widesl. |
| Days a Buy qualified | A Buy variant ranked in the overall top 10. |

Each has the same kind of usual range (10th to 90th percentile of the rolling 21-session values
over P1).

## Things to know

- **A flag is a request to look, not a fault.** The rule is doing what its criteria say; the band
  only says this is unlike what it did in the period it was fitted on.
- **P1 is the period the weights were chosen on**, re-scored with the same code as the 09:16 job.
  Sessions the journal has recorded are counted as recorded and are not forward unless on time.
- **Too little history.** Under 21 sessions, or too few rolling windows for a band, the card shows
  the bars and flags nothing.
- **A late entry or a day with no entry is not a session** here, and is left out.
