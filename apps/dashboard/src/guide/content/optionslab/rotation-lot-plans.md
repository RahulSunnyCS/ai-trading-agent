## What it is for

The card is under *Experiments* on the Rotation page, closed until you open it.

Each morning Adaptive picks three strategies, and today each book trades them at one lot each. Two
other ways of spreading the lots looked better in research, but they were chosen after looking at
those same past days, so the research figures prove nothing. This card follows all three on new
days, from **Monday 19 October 2026**, so they can be compared fairly.

It reads the picks already recorded at 09:16 and the results stored each evening. It never changes
a pick, a list, the journal or a Telegram message.

## The two books

Switch between them at the top right.

| Book | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Core** | The live Adaptive list, ranked over the 298 core strategies. |
| **T1** | The T1 track: Adaptive over the core strategies plus the T1 settings, with its own record. |

## The three plans

An **expiry day** is a day when NIFTY or SENSEX expires (its days to expiry is 0 in the morning
record). The Buy add-on is left out of every plan; if one is picked, it is extra.

| Plan | Non-expiry day | Expiry day |
|---|---|---|
| **As picked** (the yardstick) | 1 lot on each of the three picks (3 lots) | The same (3 lots) |
| **Dir Boost** | The three picks, with the Dir at 2 lots (4 lots) | As picked (3 lots) |
| **Duo** | The top Widesl at 2 lots and the Dir at 2 lots (4 lots); the other Widesl is not traded | Top Widesl 2 lots, Dir 1 lot (3 lots) |

The **top Widesl** is the Widesl pick with the higher ranking score that morning.

Some mornings all three picks are Widesl, so there is no Dir. Then Dir Boost and Duo use the **best
Dir that was not picked**: the same "optional Dir" the 09:16 Telegram message shows. Dir Boost adds
it at 1 lot beside the three Widesl; Duo uses it as its Dir. The card works it out again from the
results stored before that day and checks that it gets exactly the picks recorded that morning. If
it does not, the day is marked **Unverified** and left out, rather than guessed.

## The figures

- **Net** is each strategy's one-lot result times its lots, minus an estimate of brokerage:
  ₹20 plus 18% GST per order, two orders a trade, charged once per strategy however many lots it
  has. Other charges (STT, exchange fees) grow with lots and are left out of every plan alike.
- **Lots today** is what each plan holds on today's picks, once they are recorded.
- **Net per day**, **max drawdown** (the deepest fall of the running total from its high) and the
  **worst day** are over the scored days only. **vs As picked** is each plan's net per day minus As
  picked's.
- The chart is the running net total of each plan.

Doubling a strategy assumes its own stop doubles with it.

## Day status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scored | Every result the plans need is stored. Counted. |
| Waiting | A result is not stored: the evening run has not finished, or that day's data was excluded (then it stays waiting). Shown blank, never as zero. |
| Late | The morning record was made at or after 09:17. Shown, never counted. |
| Unverified | The best unpicked Dir could not be confirmed. Shown, never counted. |

A day counts for all three plans at once or for none, so the plans are always compared over the same
days. Point at a day's figure to see what each plan placed.

## The read-out

After 60 scored days in a book, the card compares Dir Boost and Duo with As picked: the difference
in net per day, with a 90% range for how sure that is. A plan **beats As picked** only if the whole
range is above zero and its drawdown per lot is no deeper. The card shows the dates those 60 days
span. Days after them are shown as a plain difference, without a verdict. This rule was fixed before the first
forward day, and the result changes nothing by itself; trading a plan stays the owner's decision.

> [!NOTE]
> The card works the read-out out afresh from the stored files each time. The written record is
> the note made when the 60th day is scored. If a day's status changes later (a result stored
> late, a day becoming unverified), the 60 days shown here can shift, and the figures with them.
