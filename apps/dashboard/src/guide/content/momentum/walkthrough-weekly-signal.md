This walkthrough goes from Friday's Telegram message to a portfolio that matches the model.

## 1. Read the message

On Friday the headline favourite's final signal arrives: at 16:45 (IST) for an ETF strategy, at
19:30 for a stock-based one such as the Phase 6 ensemble, once the day's stock data is in. It
lists what to **sell**, what to **buy**, and what to **top up** or **trim**. A group's message
also says which of its sleeves trade this week.

## 2. Check it on the dashboard

Open [This week](app:/momentum/week). The **Friday timeline** should show the steps done; if
**Needs attention** lists anything (data not ready, a split to classify), deal with it first.
The headline's table shows the same trades as the message, with each name's score strip and
rank.

## 3. See why

Open [Backtest](app:/momentum/backtest), load the same strategy from
[Saved runs](app:/momentum/saved), run it, and scroll to the **This week** section below the chart. You can see every
candidate's rank, and why a name was sold (its rank fell past the exit rank) or bought.

## 4. Compare with what you hold

Open [Rebalance](app:/momentum/rebalance), choose the strategy, and enter your current
holdings. The **indicative changes** are the trades that would bring you in line.

## 5. Decide

The signal is information, not an instruction. Skip trades too small to be worth the brokerage,
and remember the tax on anything sold within a year. If you trade, you do it yourself in your
broker; nothing here places orders.

## 6. Later: check the record

Over the following weeks, the [Journal](app:/momentum/journal) shows each signal exactly as it
was sent, and the next week's entry shows how it turned out.
