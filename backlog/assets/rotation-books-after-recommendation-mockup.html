What changed, in one line each

• "Beats random", "Last 60 sessions" and "Last 6 months" percentages are gone; one "vs base" column follows the window picker instead.

• The drift alarm turns the 60-session window into a rule, not a number to watch.
• The live columns and the registered verdict are untouched; the only new line is the honest note on what 60 sessions can show.
