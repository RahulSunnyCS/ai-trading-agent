## Context

The owner's plan (2026-10-06): use the Momentum tool and the options workbench with two or three
friends for two to three months, learn what to improve, and only then think about others. Without
criteria written in advance, that decision tends to follow how the last few weeks felt. Writing
them now keeps it honest.

Approved by the owner on 2026-10-06 (from the review PR #27).

## Goal

A short criteria list written now, and a recorded go / not yet / no decision against it at the end
of month 3 (about 2027-01-06).

## Plan

### Phase 1 — Write the criteria (now)
- **Tasks:** with the owner, agree measurable criteria. Suggested starting points:
  - forward results (BL-024) and realised options P&L (BL-026) for the period, against the backtest;
  - how often the owner and friends actually used it, and for what;
  - the features friends asked for that the owner would not have built;
  - what it would take to run without the laptop (BL-012 Phase 5) and with real accounts;
  - the regulatory check noted in BL-019 if signals, not just tools, would be offered.
- **Done when:** the list is committed in this file.

### Phase 2 — Decide (about 2027-01-06)
- **Tasks:** score each criterion; record the decision and the reason.
- **Done when:** the decision is in the log.
