## Context

Every variant carries AlgoTest's overall MTM stop per lot (Widesl ₹2,500, Dir ₹3,000, Buy ₹2,000), and
BL-066 showed the engine closes at the end of the 1-minute bar, so the stop overshoots on fast days. The
owner (2026-10-10 chat) asked for the whole list re-run with no per-strategy stop and DRB-6W3L2 tested
again on it: two questions, whether the stops earn their keep per family, and whether the "recent return"
edge found in BL-067/068 is really the stops truncating losers that recent return happens to avoid.

## Goal

Per family, total and drawdown with vs without the stop; DRB-6W3L2's baseline / recent-only / no-recent /
no-VIX rows on the no-stop results beside the with-stop ones.

## Out of scope

Other stop levels (a larger or smaller stop — a later block if asked), daily portfolio stops (BL-066),
any change to the live strategies.

## Plan

### Phase 0 — Pre-register
- **Universe:** the same 248 whole-day variants (BL-062 list) with `strategy.overall` removed — legs'
  own SL % and trail unchanged; lot sizing, exits (15:28 / 15:14), costs 0, square-off partial as
  before. Generated by `research/bl070/gen_variants.py` (asserts nothing else changed), run by
  `research/bl070/run_variant.py` over **2025-09-01 → 2026-10-08** (the rotation window; not BL-054's
  two years) into `research/bl070/results/`.
- **Rotation:** `rotate.py --basket DRB-6W3L2 --results research/bl070/results [--weights ...]` for the
  rows baseline 33/25/25/17, recent-only 100/0/0/0, no-recent 0/33/33/34, no-VIX 40/30/30/0, and the
  BL-069 survivor if one exists. Gross only (charges once for the final with/without pair).
- **Look-ahead check:** unchanged from BL-057; the stop's removal changes each day's P&L, not what the
  scoring reads.
- **Read-out, per family (Widesl OTM, Widesl closest-premium, Dir, Buy; mean over the family's variants):**
  the stop is *worth keeping* if removing it deepens the family's mean max drawdown by > 25% or cuts
  its mean total; it is *costing more than it saves* if the family's mean total rises with the drawdown
  within 25%.
- **Read-out, DRB:** the recent edge *survives* without stops if (baseline − no-recent) keeps its sign
  and at least half its with-stop size (₹2,04,763 gross), and recent-only still beats the random P90;
  it is *stop-dependent* if that gap falls below half. The no-stop baseline is compared with the
  with-stop baseline (₹4,30,868 / −₹68,294) on total and drawdown, reported.
- **Hold-out:** none (owner override, as BL-053 → BL-068). **Will not run:** other stop levels, stops on
  some families only, the no-stop list combined with BL-065's variants.
- **Result:** pending.

### Phase 1 — Run
- 248 engine runs, 8 in parallel (≈ 4 h); results are git-ignored like every other research tree.
- **Done when:** 248 result files with the same day set; `rotate.py --results` reproduces the with-stop
  baseline when pointed at the original tree.

## Risks

Without a stop the Dir family's re-entry rule and the Buy family's trailed stop still cap losses per leg;
only the strangles can run to the 115% leg stop on both sides. Days the engine marks `stopped_by` are
reported so the stop's actual firing rate is visible.
