# Wider-universe stock groups (C1 extended tags)

BSE sector classification (Sector > Industry > Industry Group > Sub-group), applied by `categories/wide_tags.py` to the stocks outside the 755-name Total Market that pass the liquidity gate (current classification, all years). Used only when `category_tags="extended"` (search arm C1). The curated `stock_groups.csv` is never changed.

BSE splits industries finely, so after a member-by-member review (`wide_tags.MERGES`) most BSE groups were folded into the matching original category. Result: **1101 stocks**, of which 907 joined 58 original categories and 194 sit in **13 new categories** (suffix `(wider universe)`). Extended set: 126 categories.
