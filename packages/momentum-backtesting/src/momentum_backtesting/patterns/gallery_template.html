What each detector looks for
Tight range
An uptrend (above the 30-week average, 25%+ off the 52-week low), then the last 3, 5 or 8 weeks trade in a band of at most 8 / 12 / 15% that is also no more than half the stock's usual band for that length. Pivot: the band's high.
Bull flag
A pole of 20%+ in 3–25 trading days, then 5–20 days that give back at most a third of the pole and 15% from its top, with highs not rising and volume below the pole's. Pivot: the flag's high.
Cup and handle
After a 30%+ advance, a U-shaped cup of 7–65 weeks and 12–35% depth, right lip within 10% of the left, then a 1–4 week handle in the cup's upper half, at most 15% deep. Pivot: the handle's high. Drawn in weekly bars.
