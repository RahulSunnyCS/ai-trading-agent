# Six ways to improve the Broad strategies: what the backtests say (BL-054, 2026-10-09)

**Verdict: none of the six levers is adopted.** Each was judged after tax at Rs 5 lakh on the
11 strategies from 2026-10-08 (the 10 top-ranked configs and the frozen Phase 6 ensemble),
chosen on FY2018-FY2022 and read once on FY2023-FY2026, by rules committed before any run
(`search_spaces/bl054_criteria.json`, addendum 1). Results: `search_spaces/bl054_result.json`;
per cell `data/search/round7_A/bl054/<lever>/cells_dev.csv` and `cells_conf.csv`.

| Lever | What was tested | Verdict | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Trade every 4, 6, 8 or 13 weeks; hold a gain to long-term | Kill | Slower trading saves tax but misses more of the move; the tax hold never fires in Broad |
| L2 | All rebalance Fridays at once | Report only | Removes Friday luck: the result lands mid-range, the worst case improves |
| L3 | 25% or 50% of the money in the ETF rotation | Kill | Shallower falls, but costs 5.8 points a year on FY2018-22 |
| L4 | Daily stop, sold at the next morning's open | Kill | A 25% peak stop helped on FY2018-22 and failed on FY2023-26 |
| L5 | Size each buy by inverse volatility | Kill | Costs 1.4 points a year, falls no better |
| L6 | Rank on residual momentum | Kill | As defined, it removes the momentum itself: -16 points a year |

## Baseline

Median of the 11 strategies, every rebalance phase blended (equal tranches), Rs 5 lakh.

| Window | Before tax | After tax | Third-worst FY ahead of Nifty200 Momentum 30 TRI, after tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2018-FY2022 (choose) | 38.3% | 29.9% | 4.6 pts |
| FY2023-FY2026 (confirm) | 41.2% | 32.4% | 11.6 pts |

Tax costs 8 to 9 points a year. That is the largest single number in this work.

## L1: trade less often, hold gains to long-term

Changes are medians over 11 strategies, after tax, in points, against each strategy's own
2- or 4-week cadence. Pass needed +1.5 points on FY2018-22 with Ulcer no worse than +1 point.

| Cadence | FY2018-22 CAGR | Not worse | Ulcer | FY2023-26 CAGR | Not worse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every 6 weeks | +0.9 | 7 of 11 | -0.8 | -2.4 | 0 of 11 |
| Every 8 weeks | +0.2 | 6 of 11 | -0.8 | -6.2 | 0 of 11 |
| Every 13 weeks | -0.7 | 3 of 11 | -0.9 | -8.2 | 0 of 11 |

- No cadence reached the bar. In the 2023-26 run-up, waiting longer cost far more than the tax
  it saved.
- **The tax hold (`tax_hold_band`) is inert for Broad.** It reached the engine (checked), but
  every Broad sale is an "ineligible" exit: the stock left its category or the pool. The hold
  only delays a sale caused by a rank slip, so it never applies. Cutting the tax would need a
  different rule, for example keeping a nearly year-old winner when its category drops out.

pre-registered test, so read it as a measurement. Taken forward as BL-056.

Splitting the money across every Friday does not raise the average; it removes up to 10 points
of calendar luck. Every after-tax number above (L1, L4-L6) already uses the blended version.
The own-Friday after-tax figure was not run.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## L4: daily stop, sold at the next morning's open

Every trading day's close is checked; a holding that is X below its buy price or Y below its
highest close since buying is sold at the next session's open (a lower-circuit morning delays
it). Cash waits for the next rebalance. Changes after tax, in points; positive worst-fall
change means shallower.

| Stop | FY2018-22 less pain | CAGR | Worst fall | Ulcer | FY2023-26 less pain | CAGR | Worst fall | Ulcer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25% below peak | **8 of 11** | -0.4 | +2.0 | -0.2 | 0 of 11 | -1.3 | -1.1 | +0.7 |
| 20% buy or 25% peak | **8 of 11** | -0.5 | +1.7 | -0.2 | 0 of 11 | -1.9 | -0.9 | +0.6 |
| 30% below peak | 1 of 11 | -0.8 | +0.8 | +0.5 | 0 of 11 | -3.1 | -3.3 | +1.9 |
| 20% buy or 30% peak (owner's rule) | 2 of 11 | -0.9 | +1.1 | +0.4 | 0 of 11 | -4.1 | -3.4 | +1.8 |
| 20% below buy | 2 of 11 | -0.1 | +0.5 | +0.1 | 0 of 11 | -0.8 | -0.7 | +0.1 |
| 25% below buy | 2 of 11 | +0.1 | +0.2 | +0.1 | 3 of 11 | 0.0 | -0.1 | 0.0 |
| 15% below buy | 2 of 11 | -1.8 | +0.4 | +0.4 | 0 of 11 | -2.2 | -1.5 | +0.6 |

- Three cells passed the development rule, all with a 25% peak stop: on FY2018-22 they made
  the worst fall about 2 points shallower for half a point of CAGR. None held up on FY2023-26,
  where they made the worst fall deeper and cost 1.3 to 1.9 points. **Killed.**
- The owner's 20%/30% rule costs 0.9 points on FY2018-22 and 4.1 on FY2023-26 after tax.
- Why: these strategies already exit a weakening stock through the ranking. A stop mostly sells
  a strong stock on a sharp dip; it often recovers and is bought back at the next rebalance.
  BL-053 found the same for the weekly stop.
- Re-run after the PR #152 review fixes (no re-buy of a stock whose stop is pending; a
  confirmed factor applied only to an unadjusted prevclose): every median moved by 0.1 point or
  less and the verdict is unchanged. The numbers above are the re-run's.
- How often it fires: in a spot check of Five Sectors Monthly (one phase), the 25% peak stop
  sold 23 times in ten years. The runner's per-strategy count was wrong for the first runs and
  is not reported.

## L5: inverse-volatility sizing

| Window | Less pain | CAGR | Worst fall | Ulcer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2018-22 | 2 of 11 | -1.4 | +0.4 | +0.4 |
| FY2023-26 | 6 of 11 | -0.4 | +0.2 | -0.1 |

Giving calmer stocks more money tilts the portfolio away from the fastest movers, which is
where these strategies earn. The worst fall moved only 0.2-0.4 points shallower, and overall
pain improved in just 2 of 11 strategies on FY2018-22. **Killed.**

## L6: residual momentum

| Window | Less pain | CAGR | Worst fall | Ulcer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2018-22 | 0 of 11 | -16.4 | -12.7 | +6.7 |
| FY2023-26 | 0 of 11 | -29.5 | -25.4 | +8.5 |

Checked for a bug before accepting it. The pre-registered definition (as in Blitz, Huij and
Martens 2011) regresses with an intercept, so a stock's whole-year outperformance goes into the
intercept and the score measures only its last 26 weeks against its own 52-week average. The
top 30 residual names had a median 52-week return of 3% (2020-12-25), -3% (2025-06-27) and
34% (2023-06-30), against 265%, 65% and 184% for the momentum top 30. The score removes the
momentum the strategy depends on. **Killed as defined.** A version without the intercept is a
different question and would need its own pre-registration. BL-050's residual trial (same
intercept) failed its screen too.

## What this means for the money

- Nothing tested here beats running the strategies as they are. The honest after-tax
  expectation is about 30-32% a year for the typical top config on this history, against 17%
  before tax for the momentum index: still an upper estimate (today's category tags, a search
  space shaped with hindsight, and a 2012-16 hold-out that failed).
- The biggest lever left is tax (8-9 points a year), and the existing tax-hold rule cannot
  touch it in Broad.
- A stop-loss, daily or weekly, should not be expected to improve returns. If one is wanted as
  a behavioural safety net, the 25% buy-price stop is the one whose numbers show the least harm
  (+0.1 then 0.0 points of CAGR). That cell failed the development rule (less pain in 2 of 11),
  and its FY2023-26 numbers were read outside the pre-registered gate (see the Caveats), so this
  describes what was measured; it is not a pass and not a recommendation.

## Caveats

- In-sample strategies: the 11 were selected on 2017-2026 without these levers; FY2023-26 is a
  confirmation window, not an unseen one.
- Deviation from the pre-registration: FY2023-26 was computed for every lever, not only for
  those that passed development. The kills do not depend on it (each fails on FY2018-22), but
  the window is spent as a confirmation window. Recorded, with the owner's override, in the
  Log of BL-054.
- Tags are today's curated categories applied to every year.
- 13-week cadence splits Rs 5 lakh into 13 pots of about Rs 38k, so the flat Rs 16 depository
  charge weighs more than it would live.
- Daily stops fill at the open with the itemised 15 bps slippage and nothing more; cash from a
  stop earns nothing until the next rebalance.
- Trials: 21 after-tax cells across L1 and L4-L6, plus L3's two mixes.
