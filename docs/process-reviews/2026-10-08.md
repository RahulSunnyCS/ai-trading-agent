test, every search was pre-registered and three research ideas were killed honestly rather than

The first Friday of the forward journal is tomorrow (9 Oct) and the scheduler that writes it is

## What is going well (keep doing it)

- **Research honesty.** Three ideas built, tested and killed in four days (BL-042, BL-043 twice),
  a hold-out failure recorded as a failure, PBO reported. This is the moat; most retail quant
  work never gets here.
- **Review before merge, with fixes landing.** 33 review-fix commits and the merge guard mean
  the review is not ceremonial.
- **Tests travel with features.** Goldens, a look-ahead test, scenario fixtures, e2e repair,
  and the BL-018 data-dependent test fixes.
- **Docs in the same commit.** Backlog, TODO, Guide pages and CLAUDE.md move with the code,
  as the repo rules ask.
- **CI is trusted again.** A red run now means something.

## What is going wrong, or drifting

1. **The strategy decision is being postponed by building around it.** BL-010 open question 2
   ("which strategy will be followed with real money") and the Phase 6 "paper tracking awaits
   the owner's go" are three days old. Everything in BL-049/051/052 presents results that the
   validation says are not yet trustworthy. The UI is good work; it is not this month's work.
2. **Branch protection is still off.** Every other gate exists, but a merge from the GitHub UI
   or `gh` outside a Claude session skips all of them. It is a five-minute owner task.
3. **Too many branches at once.** 54 sync merges in four days and three open BL-051 PRs that
   touch the same page (Phases 3, 3b and 5) will cost conflict time and make review harder.
   Each extra parallel branch also multiplies Claude tokens on re-reading `main`.
4. **Context is growing against the BL-021 target.** `technical.md` is 599 lines and every
   session loads it. The month ends with a move to Pro, where this is the cost that bites.
5. **Hours.** 68 commits between midnight and 04:00 IST. Four days is fine; a month is not, and
   the decisions that matter (real money) deserve a rested owner.
6. **Operational items are merged but not run.** The scheduler (BL-012), the Fyers login job,
   Postgres on :5433, the `pmset` wake: all "owner: install" in TODO 1.10. Tomorrow's journal
   write depends on them.
7. **Small hygiene.** Auto-generated PR titles ("Feat/bl 049 scores phase1"), a typo in the
   release PR, three commits with no type prefix. Cheap to fix with a PR template.
